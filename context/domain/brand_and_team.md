Orientation for the AML base layer. Every paragraph is taken from the Venclexta pack's sourced material.

**MABI — Marketing and Business Insight**, a "120-member team". Its oncology remit is
"Oncology – VEN – AML and CLL"; other umbrellas within MABI are Immunology and Endo.
*(Notes_Data Sources_1.pdf · page 5)*

The work is described by product lifecycle phase:

> Newly launched: Tracking of performance.
> Stabilized performance: Growth based analytics – In which areas and what markets.
> Maturity phase: How to keep the growth at the decent pace.

*(Notes_Data Sources_1.pdf · page 5)*

"offers patient services – Offers and discounts, refill notifications, training and education".

Data is classed as "Primary – Data gathered first-hand by market research teams" and
"Secondary – Data already available with us". This pack is entirely secondary data.

*(Venclexta Overview.pptx · slide 1; strengths confirmed by Venclexta PI.pdf · Dosage Forms and

**Mechanism of action**, verbatim: "BCL 2 Inhibition. Apoptosis: Destroys damaged and diseased
cells. BCL 2: BCL 2 proteins which are anti-apoptotic are over expressed in CLL/AML which prevents
cancer cells from dying due to apoptosis. Venclexta: Venclexta inhibits BCL 2 protein thus promoting
cancer cell death due to apoptosis." *(Venclexta Overview.pptx)*

**Approved indications**, from the prescribing information:

> "For the treatment of adult patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) or small lymphocytic
> lymphoma (SLL).
> In combination with azacitidine or decitabine or low-dose cytarabine for the treatment of
> newly-diagnosed acute myeloid leukemia (AML) in adults who are age 75 years or older, or who have
> comorbidities that preclude use of intensive induction chemotherapy. This indication is approved
> under accelerated approval based on response rates."
*(Venclexta PI.pdf · Indications and Usage)*

The onboarding deck paraphrases these as "Approved for both 1st line and 2nd line treatment" for CLL
and "Approved for 1st line treatment" for AML. The prescribing information states no such line
restriction for CLL. Both are recorded; the label is the authority. *(Venclexta Overview.pptx;
Q-69)*

### CLL versus AML — why they are analysed differently

The AML rules guide states the contrast directly, and it is the reason almost every rule in this pack
is indication-specific:

| AML | CLL |
|---|---|
| Acute, fast-progressing disease | Chronic, slow-progressing disease |
| Short treatment cycles | Long "watch-and-wait" periods |
| High early mortality | Long longitudinal journeys |

> "AML analyses require a shorter lookback and post treatment history to capture a complete cycle,
> whereas CLL needs a longer timeframe to avoid dropping patients prematurely. Applying the same
> eligibility rules across different therapeutic areas would cause: Lines of therapy to be classified
> incorrectly; Patient drop offs would be over/under inflated; Loss of data accuracy."
*(AML SHA Business Rules Guide V5.pptx · slide 16)*

This is codified as a pack rule: never apply CLL eligibility rules to AML.

**AML data starts later.** "Since VENCLEXTA was approved for AML in 2019, SHA data from 2019 onwards
is used." *(AML SHA Business Rules Guide V5.pptx · slide 6)*
